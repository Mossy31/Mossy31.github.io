Biography

elonMuschio started playing on the server with two friends, Keeby_ and Fylyberto, without even knowing how to use hacks. The three of them built a small underground base together, with Keeby_ and Fylyberto helping him construct it.

After his friends stopped playing, elonMuschio met ShrillingJay, an older and wealthier player, and built a base with him. Another player, Lucifer, later showed elonMuschio the location of his own base; elonMuschio stole some of Lucifer's items from it. When Lucifer noticed items were missing, he moved his belongings to the base elonMuschio shared with ShrillingJay — elonMuschio then took everything stored there as well, though he later returned the items to both players via teleport.

elonMuschio subsequently met AdPro_piglet, known as Macaroni, who became one of his most trusted teammates. At the time, elonMuschio was assembling a duplication stash for what he refers to as the "Bundle Dupe v3". With Macaroni's help, the two sorted a large amount of resources across several shulker boxes and went on to participate in two events at their shared base: during the Limitless Enchants Event they built a villager breeder and a large trading hall, and during the Easter Event elonMuschio built a chicken farm producing roughly 10 eggs per second.

Bans and Conduct

elonMuschio scammed Bluestone and has been banned from the server twice. The account below is based on elonMuschio's own submitted history.

The Balance Exploit
During the Easter Event's cookie-based economy, elonMuschio abused a bug in Bluestone's gambling plugin (see Balance Duplication Bug) using alt accounts and later Python bots, topping the server's balance leaderboard and winning the event.
Smuggling via SavedHotbars
After topping the leaderboard, elonMuschio asked Bluestone for building materials. He prepared the requested shulker box list himself, hiding NBT-saved chests full of high-tier items and pre-configured trading villagers inside — see NBT Chest Smuggling. Bluestone copied the boxes to him via the SavedHotbars plugin without noticing the hidden contents.
First Ban
elonMuschio left the smuggled items untouched for a couple of weeks, planning to later claim he had "found an exploit" before revealing them. He teleported a small group of friends to a base to test the items under one rule: don't tell anyone. One of them showed the items to a player known as DINO, who reported the stash to Bluestone. elonMuschio was banned; Bluestone did not find his remaining stashes and unbanned him afterward.
Second Ban
Still wanting to use his remaining stashes, elonMuschio publicly claimed he was running "100 LLM instances" to search for exploits as cover, then — less than a week after being unbanned — announced he had found a new exploit and began pulling items from another stash. Bluestone recognized the same smuggled items and banned him again, this time for longer, and built a plugin to detect and destroy illegal item stashes on the server.
Aftermath
During elonMuschio's second ban, SonOfGib reportedly located some of his remaining stashes and used the items to duplicate kits; Bluestone later discovered this and deleted the kits. Following the bans, elonMuschio's currency balance was wiped along with his rare event items — including his 16 Snowballs, Halloween event items (including the body parts), Grapple Hooks, Uprising Potions, and Gumball Machines. His enchanted books and one shulker box of rare items were not deleted. Staff member Pryda subsequently gave him several shulker boxes of basic gear. Macaroni also permanently lost all of his written lore books during this period, as no copies existed.

The Spear's History

Lost, Recovered, Lost Again
Before crafting the definitive spear, elonMuschio had a first version that became bugged and could no longer be upgraded. A player killed him at spawn and stole it. elonMuschio tricked the thief into a fake trade — offering the mace in exchange — and recovered the spear. Shortly after, a different player killed him at spawn again. The spear was not picked up by the attacker and despawned permanently. elonMuschio then crafted the definitive version during the Limitless Enchants Event.
